13 // KEY RISKS + MITIGATIONS HONEST
Telegram account bans
Main tech risk. Mitigation: 2-week warm-up for new accounts, max 20 channels/day per account, sharding (50-100 channels/account), mirror accounts as backup.
AI provider pricing/API changes
BYOK helps: users handle their own costs. Mitigation: AI Router auto-fallback to user's backup provider. Abstract behind a trait so switching providers is one config change.
Low conversion: lead → paid customer
Common SaaS problem. Mitigation: Free tier that's genuinely useful (5 channels, 20 matches/day) but clearly limited. Copilot features paywalled = strong upgrade trigger.
Legal (data, privacy)
Parsing public data is OK-ish but scraping ToS risk exists. Mitigation: Register legal entity (LLC / ИП) from day 1, clear ToS + privacy, GDPR compliance, data export on request.
Competitor moves (CARDINAL copies Copilot)
Mitigation: Speed — ship 21 features fast, build moat via data (re-ranker training on user behavior). BYOK is hard to replicate if competitor's business model relies on token markup.
AI quality / hallucinations
Generated proposals may look AI-ish. Mitigation: Voice & Tone Mimic trained on USER's past replies. Human-in-the-loop — every AI output is editable before send. Analytics show what works.
